Good to know

Quick answers for exhibitors and visitors. Anything missing? Ask us directly.

Ask on WhatsApp
What’s the difference between a booth and an empty space?

A white booth comes fitted out by us. An empty space is a 3×3 m floor area that you set up yourself, for example with your own booth or a food truck.

How many spaces can I book?

Up to three per order. Need more? Message us on WhatsApp and we’ll arrange it.

What do I need to provide?

Your project name, contact details, a logo and at least one product photo.

Where is the event?

Al Safia Park, Al Nahda, Dubai, 20–22 November 2026, from 4 to 10 pm. Parking is available at the park. See the map for directions.

How can my company become a sponsor?

Fill in the short form in the sponsors section and we’ll reply on WhatsApp with options and availability.
